About this site

Neighbourhood Events is an automatic, unofficial guide to what's on in and around Saint-Gilles. Twice a day it reads the public agenda pages of local venues and organisations and gathers everything into one place — no submissions, no accounts, nothing behind a login.

Every event links back to its source, so you can always check the original listing for tickets or details. Categories and tags are assigned automatically and can occasionally be wrong — treat this as a helpful starting point, not the final word.

Automatic neighbourhood agenda — no sign-up, no account, just what's happening near you.
